About Me

Projects

Pneumonia Classifier

Tools: Python, Tensorflow/Keras, Pytorch, Matplotlib, Numpy

To expand my skillset and remain competitive, I've developed a pneumonia classifier using convolutional neural networks(CNNs) to diagnose pneumonia from Chest X-ray images. This project was not only a technical challenge but also a meaningful application of AI in healthcare.

Before training the model, I had to preprocessing the data by standardizing image formats, normalizing pixel values so the model can interpret the data, and balancing the dataset to minimize bias.

The final model achieved 80% accuracy in detecting pneumonia cases across thousands of images. I was quite satisfied with the result, but what truly stood out was that my project unexpected reach. Numerous people found my work thorough to the point where it has been downloaded dozens of times.

This project reinforced my passion for applying machine learning to real-world problems. The experience deepened my understanding of data preprocessing, model tuning, and the importance of clear documentation. The positive reception of this project has motivated me to continue building solutions that bridge technology and impact.
